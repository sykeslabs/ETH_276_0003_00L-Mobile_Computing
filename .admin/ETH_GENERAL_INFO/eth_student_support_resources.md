# Student Support & Well-being Resources at ETH Zurich

This page collects information published by ETH Zurich about student support and well-being. It brings together ETH services, student-led support, and external helplines referenced in ETH resources. The original sources are linked below and in the [Sources](#sources) section for further details and current contact information.

If you are facing a difficult period - whether it is personal, academic, or professional - **you do not have to handle it alone**.

---

## Emergency & Immediate Safety (24/7)
If you are in immediate danger, experiencing acute violence, or facing a life-threatening mental health crisis, use these numbers right away:
* **Police:** 117
* **Ambulance:** 144
* **International Emergency:** 112
* **ETH Emergency (Threats/Violence):** +41 44 632 30 30 (or internal `2 30 30`)
* **Crisis Intervention Unit Zurich:** +41 58 384 20 00
* **Aerztefon (Emergency Psychiatrist/Doctor):** 0800 33 66 55

---

## Harassment, Bullying & Violence
ETH enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against inappropriate behavior. 
* **Respect Office:** The primary internal hub for personal counseling, case classification, and coaching regarding bullying, discrimination, and sexual harassment. 
  * [Website & Contact Info](https://ethz.ch/students/en/counselling/inappropriate-behaviour-conflict.html)
  * Email: respect@ethz.ch
* **ETH Whistleblowing System:** An encrypted, secure platform where you can report misconduct completely anonymously.
  * [Secure Reporting Portal](https://ethz.ch/staffnet/en/employment-and-work/consulting-and-coaching/support-inappropriate-behaviour-or-conflict/anonymous-notices.html)
* **SSHE Threat Management:** To report targeted intimidation, stalking, threats, or physical violence.
  * Email: threatmanagement@ethz.ch

---

## Burnout, Depression & Mental Health
If you are experiencing exhaustion, constant stress, low mood, or anxiety, reach out to professional counseling services:
* **Psychological Counseling Services UZH/ETH:** A dedicated, professional team offering free, strictly confidential short-term counseling. They help with stress, depression, exam anxiety, and can guide you toward long-term psychotherapy if needed. Available in English, German, French, and Italian.
  * [Website & Appointment Booking](https://www.pbs.uzh.ch/en.html)
* **ETH Counselling & Coaching Centre (StS):** Provides practical support for navigating academic pressure, time management, motivational slumps, and unique studying circumstances.
  * [Website](https://ethz.ch/students/en/counselling/beratung-coaching.html)

---

## Student-Led & External Helplines (Anonymous)
Sometimes it is easier to talk to a peer or someone entirely outside the university infrastructure:
* **Nightline Zurich:** An anonymous, confidential hotline and chat service run by students, for students, operating during the semester.
  * Phone: +41 44 633 77 77
  * [Website](https://www.nightline.ch)
* **Die Dargebotene Hand:** A confidential Swiss hotline offering emotional support 24/7.
  * Phone: 143
* **Heart2Heart Hotline:** English-speaking telephone support available daily from 6 PM to 11 PM.
  * Phone: 0800 143 000
