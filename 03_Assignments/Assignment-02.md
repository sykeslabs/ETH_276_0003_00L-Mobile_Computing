### Assignment 02: How Does a Mobile Network Manage itself?

This homework assignment is for PART I of our course, "276-0001-00L Wireless Data Communication HS2026", and for PART II, "276-0003-00L Mobile Computing HS2026".

This is a homework assignment. Please adhere strictly to the [Submission Guidelines](./Submission-Guidelines.md) for this course. While collaboration and interaction among participants are encouraged, students can submit their results as teams of two, just like in the last assignment. Work through the following list of Notebooks and fill in your answers to the questions at the end of the notebooks. After working through the notebooks, save and submit them as described in the submission guidelines.

**Submission deadline: Two weeks after the second class.**

**Submit:** [Assignment 02 on Moodle](https://moodle-app2.let.ethz.ch/mod/assign/view.php?id=1446576)
