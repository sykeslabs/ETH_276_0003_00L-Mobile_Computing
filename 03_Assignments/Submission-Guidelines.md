### Submission Guidelines

#### When and What to Submit

The submission deadline is shown in each assignment. If not stated otherwise, participants are required to work alone, not in a team, and submit their own individual results. Submit your completed Jupyter notebooks (`.ipynb` files) containing your answers to the questions and/or your code.

Where possible, your answers must refer to concrete values, observations, or outputs from your own notebook run. Generic explanations without run-specific evidence may receive reduced credit.

#### Use of Moodle

Submit your results through the corresponding **assignment activity in Moodle** (German: "Aufgabe"). Use the filename "`leginumber_0X`" when submitting an assignment. For example, if your legi number is `23-123-456`, the notebook for Assignment 01 should be named "`23-123-456_01.ipynb`". Make sure that Moodle confirms your submission before the deadline. Updates after the submission deadline will not be considered unless agreed otherwise.

#### No Hand-Written Texts

Please only submit typewritten results, nothing written or drawn by hand, unless specifically mentioned and permitted. This includes text written with tablets and stylus. Do not submit anything written by hand with the help of a stylus or pen.

#### Getting Help

If you experience issues, please reach out to the lecturer's team immediately. Make also use of the Moodle forum to ask questions of general interest.

#### Use of Generative AI (GenAI) such as ChatGPT
ETH Zurich advocates a proactive approach to the use of generative AI (GenAI) within educational contexts, emphasizing the responsible use of this technology. Throughout this course, the use of Generative AI and LLMs is generally permitted, within the boundaries of what ETH Zurich permits ([see here](https://ethz.ch/en/the-eth-zurich/education/ai-in-education.html)). When using AI, in each submission, clearly mark the content (text, images, code) generated by AI, and also submit all prompts that have been used.
