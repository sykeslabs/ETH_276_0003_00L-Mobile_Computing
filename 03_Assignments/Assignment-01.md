### Assignment 01: Learn How Mobile Networks Handle Data

This homework assignment is for PART I of our course, "276-0001-00L Wireless Data Communication HS2026".

This is a homework assignment. Please adhere strictly to the [Submission Guidelines](./Submission-Guidelines.md) for this course. Collaboration and interaction among participants are encouraged, each participant can submit individually, or as a team of two. Work through the following list of Notebooks and fill in your answers to the questions at the end of the notebooks. After working through the notebooks, save and submit them as described in the submission guidelines.

**Submission deadline: Two weeks after the first class.**

**Submit:** [Assignment 01 on Moodle](https://moodle-app2.let.ethz.ch/mod/assign/view.php?id=1446573)
