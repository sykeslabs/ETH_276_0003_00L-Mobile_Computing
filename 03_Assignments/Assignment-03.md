### Assignment 03: Mobile Computing Algorithms

This homework assignment is for PART II of our course, "276-0003-00L Mobile Computing HS2026".

This is the final homework assignment. Please adhere strictly to the [Submission Guidelines](./Submission-Guidelines.md) for this course. Collaboration and interaction among participants are encouraged. Participants can complete the work independently and submit their own results, or work and submit as a team of two. Work through the following list of Notebooks and fill in your answers to the questions at the end of the notebooks. After working through the notebooks, save and submit them as described in the submission guidelines.

**Submission deadline: Two weeks after the third class.**

**Submit:** [Assignment 03 on Moodle](https://moodle-app2.let.ethz.ch/mod/assign/view.php?id=1446575)

Submission is not required for notebooks marked OPTIONAL.
